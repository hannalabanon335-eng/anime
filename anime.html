Discover Popular Anime

Here are some popular anime series and their main characters.

1. Naruto

Naruto is an anime about a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage. The main character is Naruto Uzumaki.

2. One Piece

One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy and his crew as they travel the sea in search of the legendary One Piece treasure.

3. Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan is an action anime about humans fighting against giant creatures called Titans. The main characters include Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman, and Armin Arlert.

4. Demon Slayer

Demon Slayer tells the story of Tanjiro Kamado, who fights demons while searching for a way to help his sister Nezuko.

5. My Hero Academia

My Hero Academia is about Izuku Midoriya, a student who dreams of becoming a hero in a world where many people have special powers.

6. Jujutsu Kaisen

Jujutsu Kaisen follows Yuji Itadori as he becomes involved in the world of curses and sorcerers.

7. Dragon Ball

Dragon Ball follows Goku and his friends as they train, fight powerful enemies, and go on exciting adventures.

8. Sailor Moon

Sailor Moon is about Usagi Tsukino, a girl who becomes a magical guardian and fights to protect the world.
